Design Brief

To remind all students in D&T workshop the importance of health and safety standard, please design a poster about 1 to 2 safety rules on a plain page of your DT book or on an A4 paper.

You are encouraged to use visuals and typography in styles that appeal to young people as they are the target users of these posters.

Selected posters will be scanned, printed, and used in the workshop.

You can have a look at some of the examples below.

Healthy and Safety Rules

🧥

Coats and Bags

All coats and bags should be stored in the cupboard under the table to maintain a clean and safe workspace.

🏃

No Running

Never run in the D&T room. Walking helps maintain a safe environment for everyone.

✂️

Sharp Equipment

Be very careful when carrying and using sharp equipment. Handle with care and focus.

🧠

Stay Focused

Keep quiet and pay attention to what you're doing at all times.

👂

Listen to Teacher

When the teacher is talking, always listen and pay attention quietly.

❓

Equipment Knowledge

Do not use any equipment if you don't know how to use it. Ask for help!

🙋

Teacher's Permission

Always get teacher's permission before using any equipment.

1️⃣

One at a Time

Only one person at a time can use a machine.

🤫

No Distractions

Do not distract anyone who is using a machine or any other equipment.

🛑

Stop When Asked

Stop immediately if your teacher asks you to stop.
