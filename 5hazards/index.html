Matching Hazard with Risk and Control Measures

Please match each risk and control measure to a specific hazard.

Hazards

1. Craft Knife

Craft Knife: A tool with a sharp blade used for cutting materials.

2. Disc Sander

Disc Sander: A tool used for sanding surfaces to smooth them.

3. Pillar Drill

Pillar Drill: A stationary drill press used for precise drilling.

4. Hot Glue Gun

Hot Glue Gun: A tool that dispenses hot adhesive for bonding materials.

5. Scroll Saw

Scroll Saw: A tool used for intricate cutting of wood, metal, or other materials.

Risks

Cuts or lacerations

Laceration: A deep cut or tear in the skin.

Abrasions or inhalation of dust

Abrasion: A scrape caused by rubbing or scraping the skin.

Puncture wounds or flying debris

Puncture: A small hole made by a sharp object.

Burns from hot adhesive

Burn: Injury caused by exposure to heat, flame, or chemicals.

Cuts or material splinters

Splinter: A small, thin, sharp piece of material that can pierce the skin.

Control Measures

Use cutting mats and proper technique

Cutting Mat: A surface designed to protect worktables while cutting materials.

Wear goggles and dust masks

Goggles: Protective eyewear to shield eyes from dust and debris.

Use guards and secure workpieces

Guards: Safety barriers to protect users from moving parts and debris.

Use heat-resistant mats and supervise use

Supervise: Ensure proper usage of tools and safety equipment.

Use sharp blades and blade guards

Blade Guard and vice: A protective cover to shield users from sharp blades and a vice to hold the piece.
