Introduction

Materials can be categorised in different ways depending on what properties we're considering. As designers, we need to understand these categories to make informed decisions about which materials to use in our projects.

  1. Efficient Selection: Categorisation helps designers quickly narrow down suitable materials for specific applications, saving time in the design process.
  2. Performance Prediction: By understanding categories of properties, designers can predict how materials will behave under different conditions without testing every possible material.
  3. Innovation Opportunities: Categorisation highlights gaps or opportunities for new materials with specific combinations of properties that don't yet exist.
  4. Communication Clarity: Categories create a shared language for designers, engineers, and manufacturers to communicate precisely about material requirements.
  5. Sustainability Planning: Categorising materials by environmental impact helps designers make more sustainable choices and understand the lifecycle implications of their material selections.
