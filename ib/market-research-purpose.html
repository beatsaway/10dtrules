Market Research: Purpose & Consumer Reactions 📊🧑‍💼

Why do market research? How do people react to new tech and green design? 🔍

Key Vocabulary 📚

  • Market Research – Systematic gathering and analysis of data about individuals and organizations to inform decisions
  • Demographics – Statistical data about populations (age, income, etc.)
  • Market Segment – A subgroup of people with shared characteristics/needs
  • Technophile – Loves new technology, early adopter
  • Techno-cautious – Waits for proof before adopting new tech
  • Technophobe – Resists technological change
  • Eco-warrior – Actively demonstrates for environmental causes
  • Eco-champion – Champions green design in their organization
  • Eco-fan – Enthusiastically adopts green practices
  • Ecophobe – Resents environmental protection efforts

Why Do Market Research? ❓

Purpose of Market Research

  • Improve products/services
  • Identify new ideas or needs
  • Increase chances of market success
  • Set the right price
  • Understand constraints and requirements
  • Identify promotional strategies

Demographics & Market Segments

  • Demographics: age, family, income, trends
  • Market segment: group with shared needs/preferences

Consumer Reactions to Technology 📱

Technophile

  • Loves new tech, early adopter
  • Example: camping out for new iPhone

Techno-cautious

  • Waits for proof before buying
  • Majority of consumers

Technophobe

  • Resists new tech, prefers what they know
  • Often older demographic

Consumer Reactions to Green Design 🌱

Eco-warrior

  • Actively demonstrates for the environment
  • Small but passionate market segment

Eco-champion

  • Champions green design in organizations
  • Bigger segment, influential

Eco-fan

  • Adopts green practices as a consumer
  • Example: brings reusable bags

Ecophobe

  • Resents environmental protection
  • Example: "coal rolling" trucks

Fun Fact 🤔

  • Market research can reveal surprising new markets—like the rise of plant-based meat!
  • Technophobes sometimes become technophiles after trying new tech.
