Workflow Analysis, Kaizen & Empowerment 🔄👥

How do you improve processes, empower workers, and drive continuous improvement? 💡

Key Vocabulary 📚

  • Workflow Analysis – Using flowcharts to map and improve processes
  • Flowchart – Diagram showing steps in a process
  • Waste – Anything that does not add value
  • Inventory – Stock of materials or products
  • Flow – Movement of products/materials through production
  • Customer Pull – Producing based on customer demand
  • Empowerment – Giving workers the power to suggest and make improvements
  • Kaizen – Continuous, incremental improvement
  • Product Family – Group of products with common parts/assembly
  • Training – Teaching employees multiple skills/processes
  • Devolution of Power – Bottom-up improvement; frontline workers drive change

Core Concepts 🧠

Workflow Analysis

  • Use flowcharts to map processes
  • Identify areas for improvement
  • Spot waste and inefficiency

Eliminate Waste & Minimize Inventory

  • Less waste = lower costs
  • Minimize inventory to save space and money

Maximize Flow & Customer Pull

  • Keep products moving quickly
  • Produce only what customers want, when they want it

Empowerment & Kaizen

  • Train and empower workers to suggest improvements
  • Kaizen: continuous, small improvements

Product Family & Common Parts

  • Use common parts/assemblies across products
  • Improves efficiency and reduces waste

Training & Devolution of Power

  • Train employees in multiple skills
  • Encourage bottom-up improvement

Fun Facts 💡

  • Car companies often use the same chassis for multiple models to save costs and improve efficiency.
  • Empowered workers are more likely to spot and fix problems quickly.
