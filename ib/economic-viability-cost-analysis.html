Economic Viability & Cost Analysis 💸📈

How do you make sure a product is worth making? Let's break down costs, pricing, and profit! 🧮

Key Vocabulary 📚

  • Economic Viability – Can a product make money?
  • Cost Effectiveness – Most efficient way to design/produce a product
  • Value for Money – Relationship between worth and price
  • Fixed Cost – Costs that don't change with production (e.g., rent, machinery)
  • Variable Cost – Costs that change with output (e.g., materials, fuel)
  • Total Cost – Fixed + variable costs
  • Cost Analysis Tool – Tool to assess risks/gains and break-even point
  • Break-even Point – Sales needed to cover all costs
  • Pricing Strategies – Ways to set product price (market, retail, wholesale, etc.)
  • ROI – Return on Investment: profit as a percentage of money invested
  • Unit Cost – Cost to produce/store/sell one item
  • Sales Volume – Amount of product sold in a time period
  • Financial Return – Profit from a sale or investment

Concepts 💡

Economic Viability

  • Product must be able to make a profit
  • Consider all costs before launching

Types of Costs

  • Fixed: Don't change with output (e.g., rent)
  • Variable: Change with output (e.g., materials)
  • Total: Fixed + variable

Cost Analysis Tool

  • Assesses risks/gains of making a product
  • Finds break-even point

Break-even Point

  • Where total revenue = total cost
  • After this, you make profit!

Pricing Strategies

  • Market, retail, wholesale, manufacturing, target pricing
  • Must cover all costs and allow for profit

ROI & Financial Return

  • ROI: profit as % of investment
  • Financial return: profit from sales or investments

Fun Facts 💡

  • Amazon famously didn't make a profit for years—until their sales volume exploded!
  • Break-even analysis is used by everyone from lemonade stands to car companies.
