🏆 Sample HL Questions

Paper 1 (Multiple Choice)

Sample: "At which stage of the product life cycle would user attitudes and behaviours be likely to have greater impact than those of the designer or the manufacturer?"
A. Production B. Distribution C. Utilization D. Disposal
Model Answer: C. Utilization. During this stage, user behavior directly determines product performance and success, unlike earlier stages where designers and manufacturers control the product specifications.

Paper 2 (Core Material)

Sample: "Explain how relative advantage, triability and observability impact on the rate of consumer adoption of flexible screen based smartphones."
Model Answer: Relative advantage: Flexible screens offer durability and portability benefits over rigid screens, making adoption more attractive.
Triability: Hands-on testing in stores reduces uncertainty about this new technology.
Observability: Seeing others use flexible phones demonstrates real benefits, encouraging adoption through social proof.

Paper 3 (HL Extension)

Sample: "Explain how the concept of Kaizen helps to improve the efficiency of the production process."
Model Answer: Kaizen improves efficiency through continuous small improvements by all employees. This eliminates waste, reduces defects, and optimizes processes without major disruptions, leading to higher quality and faster production times.
