Quality Management: QC, QA & SPC ✅📊

How do companies make sure products are always great? Let's break down QC, QA, and SPC! 🏭

Key Vocabulary 📚

  • Quality Management – Ensuring products/services meet standards
  • Quality Control (QC) – Checking products to meet/exceed customer expectations
  • Quality Assurance (QA) – Managing processes to prevent defects
  • Statistical Process Control (SPC) – Using stats to monitor and control production
  • Shewhart Cycle – Plan-Do-Check-Act cycle for continuous improvement
  • Tolerances – Acceptable limits for product specs
  • Internal/External Customer – Next person in process vs. end user

Concepts 💡

Quality Control (QC)

  • System to ensure products/services meet or exceed customer requirements
  • Defines tolerances (e.g., size, temp, speed)
  • Samples inspected, defects reworked or scrapped

Shewhart Cycle

  • Continuous improvement: Plan → Do → Check → Act
  • Used to standardize and improve processes

Quality Assurance (QA)

  • Focuses on process, not just product
  • Proactive: aims to prevent defects
  • Includes regulation of materials, documentation, inspection

Statistical Process Control (SPC)

  • Uses statistical methods to monitor production
  • Keeps product specs within set tolerances
  • Helps spot trends and prevent defects

QC vs QA 🔍

Quality Control (QC)Quality Assurance (QA)
Product-orientedProcess-oriented
Reactive (finds defects)Proactive (prevents defects)
Checks final productChecks process throughout
External customer focusInternal & external customer focus

Fun Facts 💡

  • SPC was first used in the 1920s to improve telephone manufacturing!
  • Many companies use both QC and QA for the best results.
