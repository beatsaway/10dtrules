Market Research Strategies 🧠📈

How do designers gather the right data? Explore the main research strategies! 🔬

Key Vocabulary 📚

  • Literature Search – Reviewing existing reports, articles, and studies
  • Expert Appraisal – Getting advice from specialists
  • User Trial – Observing non-experts using a product
  • User Research – Questionnaires, surveys, interviews
  • Perceptual Mapping – Visualizing consumer perceptions on axes (e.g., classy vs. affordable)
  • Environmental Scanning – Monitoring external factors for opportunities/threats

Market Research Strategies 🛠️

Literature Search

  • Pros: Tons of data, historic perspective
  • Cons: Time-consuming, overwhelming

Expert Appraisal

  • Pros: Deep knowledge, expert advice
  • Cons: Can be biased, hard to find experts, qualitative data

User Trial

  • Pros: Real user feedback, cost-effective
  • Cons: Users may be inexperienced, qualitative data

User Research

  • Pros: Cheap, quick, can be qualitative or quantitative
  • Cons: May be biased, depends on sample

Perceptual Mapping

  • Pros: Visualizes perceptions, easy to conduct
  • Cons: Highly biased, qualitative

Environmental Scanning

  • Pros: Identifies opportunities/threats, big picture
  • Cons: Can be broad, may miss details

Fun Fact 💡

  • Perceptual mapping is used by car companies to see how people view their brand vs. competitors!
  • Environmental scanning helps companies spot trends before competitors do.
