Market Sectors & Segments 🛒📊

How companies target the right people with the right products!

Key Vocabulary 📚

  • Market sector – Broad category of the market (e.g., technology, utilities, capital goods)
  • Geographic sector – Market defined by region, culture, or climate
  • Client sector – Market defined by type of customer (public vs. private, individual vs. group)
  • Market segment – Smaller group within a sector with similar needs or characteristics
  • Segmentation – Dividing a market into groups based on demographics, lifestyle, etc.
  • Product family – Group of related products with common features or branding
  • Batch production – Making products in specific groups or amounts
  • Mass production – Making large quantities of standardized products

Core Concepts 🧠

Market Sectors

  • Broad categories: technology, utilities, capital goods, etc.
  • Help companies focus on big market areas

Geographic Sectors

  • Markets defined by region, culture, or climate
  • Example: engine block heaters in Canada, not Egypt

Client Sectors

  • Markets defined by type of customer: public vs. private, individual vs. group
  • Example: police cars (public) vs. private cars (private)

Market Segments & Segmentation

  • Segments: smaller groups with similar needs (age, income, lifestyle, etc.)
  • Segmentation: dividing the market to target products more precisely

Product Families

  • Group of related products with shared features or branding
  • Example: Coke family (Coke, Diet Coke, Coke Zero, etc.)

Production Types

  • Batch production: Limited groups, e.g., luxury pens (Montblanc)
  • Mass production: Large quantities, e.g., Bic pens

Fun Facts & Real-World 💡

  • M&M's color mix changes by country to match local preferences!
  • Montblanc pens are batch produced and can cost thousands; Bic pens are mass produced and super cheap.
  • Product families help brands reach more segments (think all the Coke flavors!).
