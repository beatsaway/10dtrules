Value Stream Mapping 🗺️📊

How do you visualize and improve every step from design to delivery? 🔍

Key Vocabulary 📚

  • Value Stream Mapping – Lean tool to analyze and improve the flow of materials and information
  • Material Flow – Movement of materials through production
  • Information Flow – Communication between customers, suppliers, and production
  • Lead Time – Total time from order to delivery
  • Value Added – Time spent actually improving the product
  • Inventory – Stock of materials or products at each stage
  • Process – Steps or activities in production
  • Customer Demand – What the customer wants and when

What is Value Stream Mapping? ❓

Definition

  • Visual tool to map every step from design to delivery
  • Shows material and information flow
  • Used to spot waste and improve processes

How It Works 🔧

Information Flow

  • Tracks communication between customers, suppliers, and production
  • Ensures the right info is available at every step

Material Flow

  • Shows how materials move through each process
  • Highlights inventory at each stage

Time Ladders

  • Visualize value added time vs. total lead time
  • Helps identify bottlenecks and delays

Value Added vs. Lead Time

  • Value added: time spent improving the product
  • Lead time: total time from start to finish

Why It Matters 🌟

Spotting Waste

  • Helps teams see where time/materials are wasted
  • Focuses improvement efforts

Continuous Improvement

  • Used in Kaizen to drive ongoing process improvements

Fun Facts 💡

  • Value stream mapping is used by companies like Toyota, Amazon, and Nike to streamline operations.
  • It can be used for services (like hospitals) as well as manufacturing!
