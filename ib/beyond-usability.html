Beyond Usability: Designing for Pleasure & Emotion 💖🎨

Great design isn't just usable—it's delightful! 😊

Key Vocabulary 📚

  • Four Pleasures – Socio, physio, psycho, and ideo pleasure
  • Socio pleasure – Pleasure from social interaction and belonging
  • Physio pleasure – Sensual pleasure from touch, taste, sound, etc.
  • Psycho pleasure – Pleasure from cognition, discovery, and achievement
  • Ideo pleasure – Pleasure from ideals, values, and aesthetics
  • Visceral design – Design that appeals to instinct and expectations
  • Behavioral design – Focuses on use, mastery, and functionality
  • Reflective design – Evokes memory, meaning, and personal stories
  • ACT model – Attract, Converse, Transact: aesthetics, interaction, function
  • Aesthetics – Visual appeal and beauty
  • Interaction – How users engage with a product
  • Function – How well a product works and solves problems

Core Concepts 🧠

The Four Pleasures

  • Socio pleasure: Belonging, socializing, group identity (e.g., gaming with friends)
  • Physio pleasure: Sensual—touch, taste, sound, satisfaction from effective objects
  • Psycho pleasure: Intellect, discovery, solving problems, achievement
  • Ideo pleasure: Ideals, values, culture, aesthetics (e.g., eco-friendly bikes)

Levels of Appeal

  • Visceral: Immediate, instinctive attraction ("I want it!")
  • Behavioral: Mastery, usability, accomplishment ("I can use it!")
  • Reflective: Memory, meaning, personal story ("It completes me!")

Visceral Design

  • Appeals to expectations and senses
  • Looks, feels, and works as expected

Behavioral Design

  • Focuses on use, function, and mastery
  • Easy to use, satisfying to master

Reflective Design

  • Evokes memory, meaning, and culture
  • Personal stories and emotional connection

ACT Model: Attract, Converse, Transact

  • Attract: Aesthetics—what draws you in?
  • Converse: Interaction—can you use it easily?
  • Transact: Function—does it work and solve your problem?
  • Great products are desirable, usable, and useful!

Real-World Examples

  • Sports fans (socio pleasure), delicious food (physio), solving puzzles (psycho), eco-friendly bikes (ideo)
  • Beautiful cars (attract), easy-to-use gadgets (converse), clever storage solutions (transact)

Fun Facts & Real-World 💡

  • Some people collect washing machines for the joy (and emotion) it brings!
  • Video games today are designed for both social and psycho pleasure.
  • Eco-friendly products often appeal to ideo pleasure—aligning with users' values.
