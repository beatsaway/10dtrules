Innovation and Markets 🚀📈

How companies grow, compete, and make an impact!

Key Vocabulary 📚

  • Ansoff Matrix – A tool for planning growth via new/existing products and markets
  • Pioneering strategy – Being first to market with a new innovation
  • Imitative strategy – Developing products similar to existing ones
  • Market development – Finding new uses or markets for existing products
  • Product development – Creating new or improved products for existing customers
  • Market penetration – Increasing sales of existing products in existing markets
  • Diversification – Expanding into new products or markets
  • Hybrid model – Using more than one strategy at once
  • Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) – Companies acting ethically for society and the environment

Core Concepts 🧠

Ansoff Matrix

  • Framework for growth: market penetration, market development, product development, diversification
  • Helps companies decide how to expand

Pioneering vs. Imitative

  • Pioneering: First to market, high risk, high reward, needs strong R&D and market research
  • Imitative: Follows existing products, less risk, focuses on branding and improvements

Market Development

  • Finding new uses or new customers for existing products
  • Example: colored duct tape for new markets

Product Development

  • Creating new or improved products for current customers
  • Example: Post-its in new sizes/colors

Market Penetration

  • Increasing sales to existing customers or finding new customers for existing products
  • Measured as product sales / total market potential
  • Strategies: promotions, celebrity endorsements, bonus offers

Diversification

  • Expanding into new products or markets
  • Example: Nike moving from shoes to all sporting goods

Hybrid Models

  • Combining multiple strategies (e.g., imitative + diversification)
  • Reduces risk, maximizes sales

Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)

  • Companies act ethically for economic, social, and environmental good (triple bottom line)
  • Examples: TOMS Shoes, Patagonia, Starbucks, Lego

Fun Facts & Real-World 💡

  • Adidas pioneered the modern running shoe; Bose pioneered noise-cancelling headphones.
  • Celebrity endorsements (like George Clooney for Nespresso) can boost market penetration.
  • CSR can actually increase sales by building customer trust and loyalty.
