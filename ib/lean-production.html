Lean Production: Principles & Practice 🍃🏭

How do companies cut waste and boost quality? Lean is the answer! 🔄

Key Vocabulary 📚

  • Lean Production – Long-term strategy to reduce waste and improve processes
  • Waste – Anything that does not add value (time, materials, effort)
  • Continuous Improvement – Ongoing, incremental improvements (Kaizen)
  • Kaizen – Japanese for "continuous improvement"; culture of small, ongoing changes
  • Just in Time – Producing only what is needed, when it is needed
  • Zero Defects – Aim for no faulty products or waste
  • Empowerment – Workers are encouraged to suggest/process improvements
  • Flow – Smooth, uninterrupted movement of products through production
  • Inventory – Stock of materials, components, or finished goods
  • Customer Pull – Producing based on customer demand, not forecasts

What is Lean Production? ❓

Definition

  • Long-term strategy to reduce waste and improve efficiency
  • Focuses on continual feedback and incremental improvement
  • Goal: reduce waste, save money, increase profits

Lean Principles 🧠

Eliminate Waste

  • Zero defects, minimize inventory, avoid overproduction

Maximize Flow

  • Keep machines and people working, avoid idle time

Customer Pull

  • Produce only what customers want, when they want it

Empower Workers

  • Workers suggest improvements, are highly trained

Continuous Improvement (Kaizen)

  • Small, ongoing changes for better quality and efficiency

Supplier Partnerships

  • Strong relationships for reliable, timely supply

Rapid Changeover

  • Processes can adapt quickly to new products or changes

Advantages & Disadvantages ⚖️

Advantages

  • Reduces costs and waste
  • Increases customer satisfaction
  • Improves quality and productivity
  • Reduces environmental impact
  • Boosts profits and competitive advantage
  • Improves working conditions

Disadvantages

  • High initial capital costs
  • Requires culture change and buy-in
  • Can cause delivery delays if not managed well
  • Supply problems can disrupt production

Fun Facts 💡

  • Lean production was pioneered by Toyota and is now used worldwide.
  • Kaizen means "change for better" in Japanese.
