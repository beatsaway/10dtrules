Market Mix: The 4 Ps 📦💸

How do designers get products to market? It's all about the 4 Ps! 🛒

Key Vocabulary 📚

  • Market Mix – The combination of factors (4 Ps) that influence how a product is marketed
  • Product – The item or service being sold
  • Price – How much the product costs
  • Place – Where the product is sold
  • Promotion – How customers are made aware of the product

The 4 Ps 🧩

Product

  • What features does it have?
  • How does it meet user needs?
  • How is it different from competitors?
  • Can it be trusted? (Standardization)

Price

  • Cost-plus pricing: add a profit margin to costs
  • Demand pricing: set price based on demand
  • Competitor pricing: price compared to rivals
  • Psychological pricing: e.g., $9.99 instead of $10

Place

  • Where is the product sold?
  • Brick & mortar stores: physical shops
  • Online: e-commerce
  • Hybrid: buy online, pick up in store

Promotion

  • Advertising: TV, online, print
  • Publicity: news, reviews
  • Personal selling: direct to customer

Fun Fact 🤔

  • The "4 Ps" model was first published in 1960 and is still used worldwide!
  • Apple Stores are a classic example of combining Place and Promotion for maximum impact.

Quick Quiz ⚡

What are the 4 Ps of the market mix?
Model Answer: Product, Price, Place, Promotion.
Give an example of psychological pricing.
Model Answer: Pricing something at $9.99 instead of $10 to make it seem cheaper.
What's a hybrid store?
Model Answer: A store that combines online and physical shopping, e.g., order online, pick up in store.
