Branding, Brand Loyalty & Value 🏷️💎

Why do brands matter? How do companies build loyalty and value? 🌍

Key Vocabulary 📚

  • Brand – A product of known source (e.g., Ford, Coca-Cola)
  • Branding – Promotion by advertising and distinctive design
  • Brand Loyalty – Preference for a supplier, even if others are cheaper or better
  • Brand Value – The financial worth of a brand (e.g., Apple, Coca-Cola)
  • Brand Ambassador – Celebrity or influencer who promotes a brand
  • Market Segment – A group of customers with shared characteristics

Core Concepts 🧠

What is a Brand?

  • Product of known source (e.g., Ford, Apple)
  • Names/logos serve as brand identity

Branding

  • Promotion by advertising and design
  • Classic example: Coca-Cola's logo and ads

Brand Loyalty

  • Customers prefer one brand over others
  • Examples: Starbucks, Callaway golf balls
  • Built through marketing, ambassadors, campaigns
  • Can be lost if market changes (e.g., Nokia, Atari)

Brand Value

  • Brands can be worth billions (Apple, Google)
  • Positive press increases value, negative press can destroy it

Brand Ambassadors

  • Celebrities/influencers promote brands (e.g., Shaquille O'Neal for Buick)
  • Appeal to specific market segments

Fun Facts 🤩

  • Apple's brand is worth more than the GDP of many countries!
  • Atari lost its brand value by releasing bad products (like the infamous E.T. game).

Quick Quiz ⚡

What is brand loyalty?
Model Answer: When a customer prefers to buy from one brand, even if others are cheaper or better.

Model Answer: Through marketing campaigns, brand ambassadors, or distinctive design.

Model Answer: Atari lost value after releasing bad games and not keeping up with the market.
