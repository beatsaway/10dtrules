Sustainable Products & Practices 🌾♻️

How can products and practices help build a sustainable future? 🌍

Key Vocabulary 📚

  • Organic farming – Growing food without synthetic chemicals
  • Genetically modified food (GM) – Food from organisms with altered DNA for specific traits
  • Forest stewardship – Responsible management of forests for sustainability
  • Bioplastics – Plastics made from renewable, plant-based sources
  • Green cotton – Cotton grown with sustainable, organic methods
  • End-of-life strategies – Plans for what happens to a product after use (e.g., recycling)
  • Bottle deposit – Refundable fee to encourage bottle recycling
  • Recycling – Processing used materials into new products
  • Product design for sustainability – Designing products to minimize environmental impact

Core Concepts 🧠

Organic Farming

  • No synthetic pesticides or fertilizers
  • Protects soil, water, and biodiversity

Genetically Modified Food

  • DNA altered for traits like pest resistance or nutrition
  • Can increase yields, but has environmental and ethical debates

Forest Stewardship

  • Managing forests to maintain biodiversity and resources
  • Certification (e.g., FSC) shows responsible practices

Bioplastics

  • Made from plants (e.g., corn, sugarcane) instead of oil
  • Can be biodegradable, reducing plastic pollution

Green Cotton

  • Organic, uses less pesticide and water
  • Better for farmers and the environment

End-of-Life Strategies

  • Designing for recycling, reuse, or safe disposal
  • Examples: tire recycling, bottle deposits

Bottle Deposits & Recycling

  • Deposit schemes boost recycling rates (up to 80%)
  • Recycling saves resources and reduces waste

Product Design for Sustainability

  • Design products to use less energy, last longer, and be easy to recycle
  • Encourages a circular economy

Fun Facts & Real-World 💡

  • Bioplastics can be compostable, but not all are—check the label!
  • Forest Stewardship Council (FSC) labels help you choose sustainable wood and paper.
  • Green cotton reduces pesticide use and protects pollinators.
