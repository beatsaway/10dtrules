Product Versions & Strategies 🎨🛒

How do companies keep products fresh and sales strong? 💡

Key Vocabulary 📚

  • Product version – Different models or types of a product for various markets
  • Product range – A set of related products (e.g., basic to premium)
  • Limited release – Special, rare versions to boost excitement and sales
  • Throwaway culture – Society's tendency to discard products quickly
  • Moore's Law – Computing power doubles every two years
  • Market segmentation – Dividing a market into groups for targeted products
  • Customization – Offering personalized options (e.g., colors, memory size)

Core Concepts 🧠

Product Versions

  • Different models for different regions/cultures (e.g., Toyota Fortuner vs. 4Runner)
  • Helps companies reach more customers

Product Range

  • Basic to premium options (e.g., Toyota Corolla vs. Camry)
  • Captures different market segments

Limited Releases

  • Special, rare versions (e.g., rare sneakers, LOL dolls)
  • Creates excitement and urgency

Throwaway Culture

  • Society's habit of quickly discarding products
  • Leads to more waste and faster product cycles

Strategies to Extend Product Life

  • New versions, colors, or models
  • Limited editions and special releases
  • Customization (e.g., memory size, color)
  • Targeting different market segments

Fun Facts & Real-World 💡

  • Toyota uses different names for similar cars in different countries!
  • Limited edition sneakers and toys can sell for huge amounts online.
  • Moore's Law means your phone gets outdated fast!
  • Throwaway culture is a big challenge for sustainability.
