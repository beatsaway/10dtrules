Strategies for User Research 🔍🧑‍🤝‍🧑

How do you really get to know your users? 💡

Key Vocabulary 📚

  • User population – The range of users for a product or system
  • Classification – Grouping users by age, gender, physical condition, etc.
  • Persona – Fictional profile of a primary target user
  • Secondary persona – Profile of a non-primary user whose needs should also be met
  • Anti-persona – Profile of someone who would not use the product
  • Scenario – Imagined sequence of events in a persona's daily life
  • Best/average/worst case – Types of scenarios for different outcomes
  • Abstraction – Level of detail in a scenario (general vs. specific)
  • Niche – A specific, often unfilled, market segment

Core Concepts 🧠

User Population

  • The range of users for a product or system
  • Can be specific (e.g., children) or broad (all ages)

Classification

  • Group users by age, gender, physical condition, etc.
  • Allows for targeted feedback and insights

Personas

  • Fictional characters representing primary users
  • Help designers empathize and focus on user needs

Secondary Personas

  • Not the main target, but their needs should be met
  • Example: Divers using baby shampoo for masks

Anti-Personas

  • Profiles of people who would not use the product
  • Helps clarify who is not being designed for

Benefits of Personas

  • Prioritize design, focus on user goals
  • Identify opportunities and test concepts
  • Visualize data as real people

Persona Pitfalls

  • Personas should not replace real user research
  • Beware of bias and assumptions

Scenarios

  • Imagined sequence of events in a persona's life
  • Shows how users interact with a product

Best/Average/Worst Case

  • Best: everything goes right
  • Average: typical experience
  • Worst: things go wrong

Abstraction

  • General scenarios = high abstraction
  • Detailed scenarios = low abstraction

Fun Facts & Real-World 💡

  • Personas are often based on real user interviews and observations.
  • Secondary personas can reveal unexpected uses for products!
  • Scenarios help teams spot design flaws before building anything.

Quick Quiz ⚡

What is a persona?
Model Answer: A fictional profile of a primary target user, used to guide design decisions and user research.
Why should you be careful not to rely only on personas?
Model Answer: Because they are based on assumptions—real user research is needed to avoid bias and mistakes.
What is a scenario in user research?
Model Answer: An imagined sequence of events in a persona's daily life, showing how they interact with a product.
