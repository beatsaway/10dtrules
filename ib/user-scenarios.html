User Scenarios 📝🧑‍💻

Imagine your user's journey—before you build! 🚀

Key Vocabulary 📚

  • Scenario – An imagined sequence of events in a persona's daily life
  • Best case – Everything goes right in the scenario
  • Average case – Typical, expected experience
  • Worst case – Things go wrong in the scenario
  • Abstraction – Level of detail (general vs. specific)
  • Scenario mapping – Visualizing user journeys and system interactions

Core Concepts 🧠

What is a User Scenario?

  • An imagined story of how a persona interacts with a product or system
  • Shows steps, decisions, and possible outcomes

Purpose of Scenarios

  • Helps designers understand user needs and pain points
  • Spot design flaws before building

Best/Average/Worst Case

  • Best: everything works perfectly
  • Average: typical experience, some minor issues
  • Worst: major problems or failures

Abstraction

  • High abstraction: general, less detail
  • Low abstraction: specific, detailed steps

Scenario Mapping

  • Visual tool to map out user journeys
  • Shows interactions, decisions, and system responses

Examples

  • Ordering food online: best case—everything arrives as ordered; worst case—order is wrong or missing items
  • Using a kettle: best case—easy to pour; worst case—too heavy for user with arthritis

Pitfalls

  • Scenarios are based on assumptions—always validate with real users
  • Too much abstraction can miss important details

Fun Facts & Real-World 💡

  • Scenario mapping is used by top tech companies to design better apps and services.
  • Worst-case scenarios help teams prepare for real-world problems before launch.
  • Scenarios can be written, drawn, or even acted out!
