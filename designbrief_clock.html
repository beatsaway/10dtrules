Design Brief – Model Answers

1. What exactly are you being asked to design and make?

A bold, pop-art styled wall clock using MDF and acrylic, designed for display in Pallant House Gallery.

2. How long do you have to complete your prototype?

Two months to produce a neat, well-made indoor display prototype.

3. Who will be using or viewing your clock in the gallery?

Gallery visitors including tourists, students, professionals, and pop art enthusiasts.

4. Where will your clock be displayed, and why does that matter?

It will be displayed indoors in each exhibition room, so it must be suitable for gallery conditions and visible to visitors.

5. What visual style should your clock reflect? Why?

A bold, playful style inspired by pop culture trends of the new generation, to fit the pop art theme and appeal to the target audience.

6. Besides looking good, what functional requirement must your clock meet?

It must clearly show the time using provided clock hands and battery pack, with easy readability.

7. What materials will you be using to construct your clock?

A base of MDF, a top layer of acrylic, and standard clock components (hands and battery pack).

8. What is the size of the clock, and how will you put it together?

The clock will be 19cm by 19cm. A hole is drilled in the center for the clock hands, with acrylic mounted on MDF.

9. What features or limitations do you need to consider when making the clock?

Design must fit provided materials and components, be durable enough for indoor display, and meet gallery standards.

10. How can you make sure your design appeals to a wide range of visitors, especially those interested in pop art?

By using vibrant colours, iconic imagery, and clear, fun design elements inspired by modern pop culture and classic pop art.
